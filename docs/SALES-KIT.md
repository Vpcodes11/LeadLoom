# Leadloom sales kit: finding the first 10 paying agencies

Everything here is copy you can paste. Replace anything in `{curly braces}`. Never add numbers or testimonials you don't have; add real ones as soon as customers give them to you.

## 1. Who to contact

**Ideal customer:** the owner or founder of a marketing, SEO, web design or branding agency with **2–20 people** that has **retainer clients** and either already sells content or gets asked for it.

**Where to find them**
- LinkedIn search: `founder OR owner` + `marketing agency` / `SEO agency` / `web design agency`, 2–50 employees. Prefer people who posted in the last 30 days.
- Clutch.co and Agency Vista listings for small agencies in your target country.
- Facebook/LinkedIn groups for agency owners, and the r/agency and r/SEO communities (give value first; don't spam).
- Penmo-style content services: agencies that resell content often mention "white-label content" on their site.

**Signals they're a good fit**
- Their site offers "content marketing", "blogging" or "SEO content".
- They post about freelancer problems, hiring writers or scaling delivery.
- They have case studies for 5+ clients (they have retainers).

**Goal for week one:** 50 personalised messages → 10 replies → 5 demos → 2 trials on real clients.

## 4. The 15-minute demo

1. **Their problem (3 min).** "Walk me through what happens when a client asks for a blog post." Listen. Write down their words.
2. **Their client, live (8 min).** Before the call, add one of *their* real clients as a brand using public info from the client's website (about page, a blog post or two as voice samples). On the call:
   - show the voice profile ("does this sound like them?"),
   - plan a funnel from the client's real goal,
   - open a finished draft and the quality score,
   - send the review link to *their* phone.
3. **The math (2 min).** Ask what they charge clients for content today, and show what's left after the plan.
4. **Next step (2 min).** "Want to run this on that client for real this week? The trial has 5 pieces. I'll check in Friday."

**After the demo:** check in on day 3 and day 7. Ask: "Did the client approve anything? What did you have to change?" Their answers are your product roadmap.

## 5. Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We just use ChatGPT." | "Most agencies do. ChatGPT doesn't remember 20 clients' voices, doesn't plan a funnel, doesn't flag invented stats, and your client can't approve in it. Try both on the same client and compare." |
| "AI content is bad for SEO." | "Google's issue is unhelpful content, not AI. That's why Leadloom flags AI-sounding phrases and unsourced claims, and why you approve every piece. You stay the editor." |
| "My clients won't want AI." | "They'll see a draft in their voice that you approved. On the agency plan your client portal shows only your name." |
| "Too expensive." | "What does one blog post cost you from a freelancer today? The agency plan works out to under $3 a piece." |
| "Not now." | "Totally fair. Can I check back in {month}? In the meantime the free trial doesn't expire." |

## 6. What to track

A simple sheet with one row per agency: name, contact, source, date contacted, replied (Y/N), demo date, trial started, client used, first draft approved (Y/N), paid (plan), notes.

The numbers that matter in month one:
- **Reply rate** (target 15%+). If lower, change the first message.
- **Trial → first approved draft** (target 50%+). If lower, the AI quality or onboarding needs work, not the pitch.
- **Trial → paid** (target 20%+).

When you get the first "this saved me time" from a real customer, ask permission to quote them, and add it to the landing page and every message above.
